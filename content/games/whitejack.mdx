Most deckbuilders are about making your deck stronger. WhiteJack is about
making yours worse on purpose. You sit down as a legendary high roller stuck
with a rigged championship deck so overpowered that almost every hand blows
straight past 21.

## Sabotaging an overpowered deck

Built for GGG Jam around the theme **Overpowered**, the game flips the
usual roguelite power curve upside down. You play through 9 antes with three
hands per ante and three chips to lose, trying to beat the dealer without
busting.

## The De-Escalation Desk

Between antes, you spend your winnings to dismantle your own deck and strip
away the advantages hurting you:

<Points>
  <Point title="Shred & stamp">Destroy high cards outright or stamp Kings down to lower values like 3s.</Point>
  <Point title="Nerf stickers & perks">Apply nerf stickers and strip away VIP badges that push your total too high.</Point>
  <Point title="Boss dealers">Test your weakened deck against rule-bending dealers like The Red-Suit Auditor, The Speed Dealer, and The Purist.</Point>
</Points>

## Balancing the first encounter

The hardest design problem was getting more than half of players past the
first encounter. In the early prototype, **no one** got past Boss 1 because
chips lost in earlier hands left players with too little room to survive the
boss. Adding health recovery after each completed encounter finally gave
players the breathing room to reach the De-Escalation Desk and play the real
loop.

## Finding the hook

I nearly gave up two days into the one-week jam. My first brainstorm fell into
the same "HR interview" interpretation of **Overpowered** that many other
entries were making, and two days passed with nothing built. Stepping back to
reframe the theme as a premise led to WhiteJack: what if your deck starts *too*
good to play?

Going forward, I want to keep widening my game references and theme-mapping
process so I can spot stronger hooks faster. Early player feedback on the jam
build has been encouraging (*"Mantap ini gamenya polished banget keren"*).
