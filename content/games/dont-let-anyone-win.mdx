export const metadata = { title: "Don't Let Anyone Win" };

You work the night shift in a drab back office as a lottery operator. The
mechanical ball machine draws honest numbers from 00 to 99, but your actual
job is the opposite of winning: make sure no claimant is ever confirmed to
take the jackpot on your watch so you can pocket the cash.

## The draw is honest, the math is rigged

Created for the Unity Game Jam around the theme **Lucky Number**, the game
flips the usual lottery fantasy upside down. Instead of hoping your ticket
hits, you are racing the broadcast timer to stop anyone else's from counting.
