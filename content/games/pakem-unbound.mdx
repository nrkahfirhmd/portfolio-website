You play as Gadjah Mada, rising from a Bhayangkara guard to the Mahapatih
of Majapahit on a Javanese Wayang Kulit stage. In traditional wayang theater,
every performance obeys the **Pakem**, the sacred script enforced by the
Dalang. Here, that script has already decided you die, so the only way to
survive is to break the rules of the stage itself.

## Fighting inside the Pakem

Built for Game Jam Plus 2026 around the themes **Roots & Legends**, **Rules
Are Made to be Broken**, and **Small Actions, Big Impact**, the game turns a
physical shadow-puppet theater into both the arena and the user interface.

## A stage instead of menus

Rather than digital sci-fi overlays, every piece of the UI belongs to the
wayang performance:
