You wake up on cold stone in a five-cell jail, guided only by a fallen knight.
To open the great gate and escape, you have to defeat the wardens, collect
five green sapphires, place them on the altar, and face what waits behind the
final door.

## No grind, pure mechanics

There are no stats, loot tables, or leveling up. Every fight comes down to
reading the room, timing your dodge, and answering with the right strike.

## Three shades of green

The entire game is rendered with ASCII glyphs in three shades of green: Deep
Moss, Sapphire Core, and Pale Jade. There is no red anywhere on screen, so
danger and safety are communicated through motion, shape, and contrast instead
of a warning color.

## The five wardens

Each sapphire is held by a warden built around a distinct spatial problem:

<Points>
  <Point title="The Turnkey">A locked core guarded by orbiting, floating keys.</Point>
  <Point title="The Pendulum">Sweeps the arena with a wide crescent blade.</Point>
  <Point title="The Prism">An honest duelist that leaves crystals behind to bend beams.</Point>
  <Point title="The Hourglass">A race against a shrinking floor and a ticking clock.</Point>
</Points>

Collect all four, place them on the altar, and the final door opens to **The
Mirror**, a boss that turns your own abilities back against you.
