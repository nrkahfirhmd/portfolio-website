## Why a gateway

Five services had five URLs, five auth flows, and five sets of logs. A single
entry point collapses that surface into one stable contract without forcing the
services to agree on anything except an upstream port.

> The gateway is not the product. It is the one door the product agrees to open.

Routing stays boring: a small table maps path prefixes to upstreams. Everything
that used to be scattered — auth, rate limits, tracing — now lives in one
middleware chain instead of five copies.
