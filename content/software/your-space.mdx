People who journal often love looking back at what they wrote months ago.
Seeing how you handled a hard week brings real perspective, like realizing
you have been in that spot before and made it through. The problem is that
most journals turn into long, cluttered walls of text, so opening old entries
feels overwhelming instead of helpful.

Your Space is built around **rereading** your own journal.

## What user testing changed

Early user tests showed that the look-back and summary experience needed work
to carry the app's value. Testers found the AI tone stiff, were confused by
icons (mistaking the filter button for list view), and felt an emotion-plus-intensity
slider missed how complex real feelings are.

## How it's built

Built natively for iOS with **SwiftUI** and **SwiftData**, using **Apple
Intelligence's on-device Foundation Models** for private summarization and a
plain **Swift** backend.

<CraftList>
  <CraftItem title="On-device AI insights">Apple Intelligence Foundation Models run on-device so journal entries stay private while generating concise insights and reflection prompts.</CraftItem>
  <CraftItem title="Grounded, natural tone">As the Applied iOS AI Engineer, I refined the AI flow so summaries stay tied to what the user actually wrote instead of sounding stiff or rambling.</CraftItem>
  <CraftItem title="SwiftData + Swift backend">SwiftData powers fast local persistence alongside a plain Swift backend.</CraftItem>
