Crypto that sits in a lending pool earns yield, but the moment you want to
pay for something with it, you have to pull it out, swap it, and send it,
by hand. DeQRypt makes it two moves: scan a QR invoice and pay straight from
your DeFi position. Alongside it, an AI agent helps put idle crypto to work,
without ever moving it on its own. We built it for the Base Hackathon.

## The agent

Ask in plain language, like "invest my idle ETH", and the agent works out
what you mean with LangChain and Gemini. It then looks up live APYs for
trusted lending pools on Base, such as Moonwell and Aave V3, from DefiLlama,
and recommends one for your token, with what you'd earn over two months, six
months, and a year.

## Consultant mode

An agent that can move money is only as safe as what stops it. In consultant
mode, the agent never lends on its own: it can only recommend. That rule
lives in two places, the prompt and the code, so even if the model is talked
into trying, the backend won't let the move through.

## My part

I was one of the two AI agent architects and backend engineers on a team of

a recommendation, and built the API the app needed around it.

## Where it ended

We didn't make the finals. DeQRypt runs on Base Sepolia, Base's test
network, with test tokens; moving to mainnet and real protocol integrations
was the next step on the roadmap.
