A farmer's week is planned around the weather: when to plant, water, spray,
or harvest. AgriClime is an Android app that helps them plan ahead, built as
our capstone project at Bangkit Academy: it forecasts the weather for the
farmer's location and labels the conditions coming.

## How the forecast works

Two models, one after the other. An LSTM looks at the last day of hourly
readings for the location and forecasts what comes next, across every
reading a weather app gives you. A TensorFlow neural network then labels the
conditions ahead, so the app can say more than numbers.

The forecast can run as far ahead as you ask, but it gets less accurate the
further out it goes, the usual trade-off with any weather model.

## The data

The models were trained on open data: the Global Weather Repository on
Kaggle, a daily-updating dataset of weather around the world. Live readings
for the app come from WeatherAPI.com.
