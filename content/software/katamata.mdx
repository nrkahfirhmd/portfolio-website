Living abroad means running a life in a language you don't speak yet: the
landlord, the market, the clinic. A dictionary gives you a word, but not
when to use it. KataMata is for expats picking up the everyday language of
the country they live in, one useful word at a time.

## What it does

<Points>
  <Point title="Collect words">Save the words you run into in daily life, building a collection that's yours.</Point>
  <Point title="Translate and explain">Each word comes with a translation and a plain explanation of what it means.</Point>
  <Point title="See it in use">An example of how the word is used, so it's ready for the next real conversation.</Point>
</Points>

## How it's built

A SwiftUI app. The explanations and examples are generated with Apple's
Foundation Models framework, so the language help comes from the model
built into the device.
