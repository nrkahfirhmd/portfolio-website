Himatif is the student association (*himpunan*) for Informatics Engineering
at Universitas Padjadjaran. Its superapp is where the association's records
live, and 300+ people use it. Version 2 is a freelance rebuild of that app
on a current stack.

## Why rebuild it

The first version ran on Laravel, on an old version of the framework, and it
had become slow. Rather than keep patching it, we rebuilt it on the current
generation of tools.

<Points>
  <Point title="v1">Laravel, on an outdated version, and slow.</Point>
  <Point title="v2">Next.js for the app, Express.js for the API.</Point>
</Points>

## What it manages

<Points>
  <Point title="Members">The association's member records, kept in one place.</Point>
  <Point title="Competitions">The competitions the association runs and takes part in.</Point>
  <Point title="Events">Its events, alongside the rest of its records.</Point>
