Every new batch starts at orientation, and in 2025 that meant more than
5,000 students arriving at once. Prabu 2025 was the website they did it
through: where they met the university, studied, and handed in their work.

## What it does

<Points>
  <Point title="Meet the university">A profile of the university for the students joining it.</Point>
  <Point title="Learn the material">The orientation's learning material, in one place.</Point>
  <Point title="Hand in tasks">Where students submit their orientation tasks.</Point>
</Points>

## How it's built

A Next.js site, built to serve the whole incoming batch.
