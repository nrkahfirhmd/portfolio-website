## How it's built

Orientation traffic spikes hard right before a task deadline. To keep the
site fast, we tuned cached Next.js pages to load in about 0.2ms on average.
For task submissions, we connected the forms to **Google Sheets and Google
Drive** through the Google API. Letting Google handle storage meant we didn't
have to build custom file servers from scratch, and student data stayed safe
under heavy traffic.

<CraftList>
  <CraftItem title="First self-hosted Docker deploy">This was my first time deploying outside Vercel or Railway. Getting CI/CD working took a few tries, especially writing a custom Docker entrypoint so environment variables loaded properly during the build.</CraftItem>
  <CraftItem title="Sorted submissions via Google API">Our form handler sent student entries straight to Google Sheets and sorted uploaded files into Google Drive folders, so the committee wouldn't have to dig through raw uploads.</CraftItem>
</CraftList>

## Why we switched to Google Form

Right near launch, university stakeholders asked us to drop the custom task
form and use a manual Google Form instead. We pushed back at first because our
automated handler already organized every file for the committee. In the end,
we went with the stakeholders' call, swapped in the Google Form, and shipped on
time.
