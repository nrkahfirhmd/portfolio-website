Booking someone to rearrange your furniture or redo your lighting usually
means chasing vendors one chat at a time. Dekorin puts them in one place: a
marketplace where customers browse home-decoration services and book them,
and vendors run their orders from their own dashboard. We built it for the
Information System Competition at Diesco 2024.

## How it's built

A Laravel app with Blade templates, in PHP.

## My role

I was the project manager, and one of the two developers. On the code side I
built authentication and the data flow between customers, vendors, and
their orders.

## The hard part

Five people, two of them developers, and no AI tools to lean on. The hardest
part wasn't the code but the scope: deciding what the requirements actually
were, and keeping everyone's expectations in line with what two developers
could ship in time.

We didn't make the finals.
