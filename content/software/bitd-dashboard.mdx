BITD is an internet provider in the Jakarta area. The BITD Dashboard is the
platform it runs on: customers, packages, invoices, support tickets, and
subscribers provisioned straight onto its routers. The same platform hosts
other ISPs as tenants, each with its own branded site.

## From the dashboard to the router

Staff create the customer in the dashboard and the platform does the rest,
over RADIUS, straight onto the MikroTik routers.

## One platform, many ISPs

Every ISP on the platform runs on its own. When a new one joins, a spawner
on the VPS sets up a fresh instance for it, with its own branded landing
site, kept apart from every other tenant.

  steps={[
