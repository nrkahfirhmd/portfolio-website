Grand Summit is StudentsxCEOs Bandung's annual event, and its 14th edition
ran two case competitions for undergraduates from national and international
universities. This was its website: where teams learned about the
competitions, registered, and kept track of their entry.

## What it does

<Points>
  <Point title="Two competitions">Pages for the Business Case Competition and the Mini Case Competition, with benefits, timelines, and case collaborators.</Point>
  <Point title="Guided registration">A seven-step form that walks a team through technical instructions, each member's details, document upload, the agreement, and a final preview.</Point>
  <Point title="Participant dashboard">After logging in, a team sees its registration and submissions in one place.</Point>
</Points>

## How it's built

Two parts in one repo. The site is Next.js. Behind it, an Express.js API
handles registration, login, and the dashboard, with sessions signed by
JWTs. Entries are written to Google Sheets and uploaded documents go to
Google Drive.

That last choice was the committee's, and a deliberate one: it hands
registrations straight to RDM, the Registration and Database Management
division, in tools they already work in, with no admin panel to learn.
