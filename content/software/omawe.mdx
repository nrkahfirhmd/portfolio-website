Getting a group to the same place is a loop of the same three moves: open
Maps, text someone "where are you?", guess their ETA, repeat. It gets worse
as the group grows, and the answers are rarely true ("five minutes away").
Omawe replaces that loop with one shared, glanceable view of everyone's
progress toward a single destination.

## What it does

<Points>
  <Point title="One trip, one place">Create or join a trip around a single shared destination.</Point>
  <Point title="Status at a glance">Live Activities on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island show who's en route, delayed, or arrived, with ETAs, no unlocking needed.</Point>
  <Point title="Sharing that ends">Location sharing is opt-in per trip, clearly scoped, and stops on its own when the trip is over.</Point>
</Points>

## How it's built

A SwiftUI app with an ActivityKit widget extension for the Live Activities,
sharing its types with the app so both sides read the same trip state. Two
details shape how it behaves on a real trip:

- **Honest under bad signal.** When GPS or the network drops, Omawe shows
  "last seen X ago" instead of a status that looks live but isn't.
- **Easy on the battery.** Background location and Live Activity updates are
  tuned so a full trip doesn't drain the phone.

Version 1 is iOS-only, built around Live Activities. Android support,
multi-stop trips, and persistent friend groups are being considered for
later versions.
