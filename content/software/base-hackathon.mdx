DeQRypt started as a hackathon MVP on Base: pay by scanning a QR code, borrow
against what you hold, and manage the lot by asking an agent in plain language
instead of clicking through a DeFi dashboard.

## What it does

<Points>
  <Point title="Scan to pay">QR payments in mUSDC and mIDRX, settled through an atomic payment router.</Point>
  <Point title="Lend and borrow">A multi-asset lending pool deployed on Base Sepolia.</Point>
  <Point title="Ask, don't click">A LangChain + Google Gemini agent that manages a portfolio from natural-language requests.</Point>
</Points>

## How it's put together

The contracts hold the money logic; everything else reacts to them. A
Python/FastAPI backend listens for on-chain events and bridges them to the
off-chain side, where the agent and the web app live.

<Figure caption="Architecture diagram goes here: contracts → event listener → FastAPI → agent and web app." />

## What's next

The roadmap moves it from testnet MVP to a real product: a third-party audit
of the lending pool and router, aimed at reentrancy and liquidity exploits,
then migrating everything from Base Sepolia to Base Mainnet.
