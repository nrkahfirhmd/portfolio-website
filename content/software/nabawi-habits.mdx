Festida, the festival at Universitas Darussalam Gontor, ran a landing page
design competition. We built Nabawi Habits: a single page on living the way
the Prophet Muhammad did, for a healthy heart, body, and mind, with every tip
paired with the hadith or Qur'an verse it comes from.

## Why this topic

The brief was open: design a landing page. We picked a topic that felt close
to us, and one that fit the organizers, an Islamic university.

## What it does

<Points>
  <Point title="Heart">Habits for a healthy heart, with their sources.</Point>
  <Point title="Body">Habits for a healthy body, with their sources.</Point>
  <Point title="Mind">Habits for a healthy mind, with their sources.</Point>
</Points>

## My part

I built most of the frontend, in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and owned
how the page moves and feels: the interactions, the animations, and the UX.

The piece I'm proudest of is a section that switches between day and night.
I worked it out on my own, before AI tools were part of how I build.
